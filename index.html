About

Design is thinking made visible.

I'm a graphic designer with a passion for building visual systems that feel intentional and human. From brand identity to editorial layout and packaging, I work at the intersection of form and meaning.

Every project starts with a question: what does this need to communicate, and who does it speak to? The answer shapes everything — color, type, space, and structure.
